The City of Redmond's all-ages Halloween party lands on **Monday, October 26**, at the Redmond Senior & Community Center. Expect costumes, a photo booth, face painting, crafts, pumpkin decorating (bring your own pumpkin), mini games, and Halloween snacks.

This is squarely a neighborhood event: the center is on 160th Ave NE, a short drive or an easy bike or walk from downtown Redmond. It's a low-key, low-cost way to get into the Halloween spirit on a weeknight, especially if there are kids around. A third-party listing puts it at 6 to 7:30 p.m. for roughly $5–6 a person, but the city requires registration, so confirm the time and price on the [city's registration page](https://app.amilia.com/store/en/city-of-redmond/shop/activities/7270109) before you go.

If you want more Halloween, the same city newsletter also lists a Trick or Treat and Haunted House at the Redmond Community Center at Marymoor Village on October 24, and a morning Trick or Treat at the Farm at Farrel-McWhirter Park on October 31 for kids ages 4 to 12.
