The city's small-scale answer to the pumpkin-patch circuit: the **Harvest Festival** at Farrel-McWhirter Park, **Saturday, October 17, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m.** Expect tractor-pulled wagon rides, fresh-pressed cider, farm animals, a scavenger hunt, potion making, and plenty of photo spots. Kids 12 and under can also get a pony ride and a pumpkin.

The park is a roughly 10-minute drive east of downtown Redmond, and it's a lot less hectic than the big farms out in Carnation or Woodinville. Registration runs through the city's recreation portal, linked from [redmond.gov/ParksRecreation](https://www.redmond.gov/ParksRecreation). Same day as the Howl-O-Ween parade at Redmond Town Center, so you can do both.
