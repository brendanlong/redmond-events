**Garden d'Lights** is the Eastside's best-known holiday light show: about half a million lights shaped into flowers, critters and waterfalls along the winter paths of the **Bellevue Botanical Garden**. On **Tuesday, December 1**, Gearhouse is going as a group on one of the garden's **$5 nights**. That's half the usual $10 online price, and the crowd is usually quieter than on a weekend.

Gearhouse members are riding the 2 Line over from Seattle. For Redmond readers this one is easy, and the train runs your way too. You can take the **2 Line from Downtown Redmond station to Wilburton** in about 15 minutes, then walk about a mile uphill to the garden or catch Bellevue's free BellHop shuttle. Driving takes about **15 minutes**, though the garden lot ($5) fills up. Wilburton Hill Park, a quarter-mile east, has free parking. The group meets at the **garden gate by 6:15 p.m.** for a 6:30–7:00 entry window and walks the roughly three-quarter-mile lit loop until about 7:30.

Tickets are online only, sold for a half-hour entry window, and non-refundable (you can reschedule or give yours away). Gearhouse confirms its five-member minimum before asking anyone to buy. The paths are gravel with some gentle slopes and plenty of puddles, so wear shoes with grip. The lights stay on in the rain. Dogs aren't allowed. RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/). If that night doesn't work, Garden d'Lights runs nightly from late November through January 3, and other dates are on the [garden's site](https://bellevuebotanical.org/garden-dlights/).
