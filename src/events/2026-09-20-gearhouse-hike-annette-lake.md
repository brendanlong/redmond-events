Another intermediate group hike from Gearhouse: **Annette Lake** on **Sunday, September 20**. It's a 7-mile out-and-back with about 2,000 feet of elevation gain through second-growth forest to a subalpine lake tucked below Silver Peak — a solid half-day workout that rewards you with a genuinely pretty swimming spot at the turnaround.

Like the club's other I-90 corridor hikes, this is an easy weekend yes for us: the trailhead sits off **I-90 Exit 47** near North Bend, away from the city rather than into it. Bring $5 cash or a Recreation Pass for the trailhead parking lot.

The **time listed above is when to meet the group at the trailhead** — the carpool leaves Gearhouse's Capitol Hill shop at **9 a.m.**, and it's about a 70-minute drive up I-90, so plan to be at the trailhead around **10:10**. Driving straight there from the Eastside skips the backtrack into the city for the shuttle.

This one's listed for Gearhouse's Summit-tier members, so double-check your membership level before RSVPing. Bring trail shoes, plenty of water, and a swimsuit for the lake.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) to confirm the meet time and carpool details.
