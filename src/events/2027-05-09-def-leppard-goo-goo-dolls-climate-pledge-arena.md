Mark your calendar early: Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inductees **Def Leppard** and alt-pop-rock mainstays the **Goo Goo Dolls** are co-headlining an arena tour that stops at Climate Pledge Arena on **Sunday, May 9, 2027** (Mother's Day weekend). Tickets went on sale this week, per EverOut Seattle's on-sale roundup.

It's a double dose of big-chorus nostalgia, pairing the hair-metal hit machine behind "Pour Some Sugar on Me" with the band behind "Iris" and "Slide". Readers with broad taste, from power metal to Death Cab-style indie, will probably find something to sing along to. The start time above is an estimate; confirm doors and set times with the venue.

Seattle Center is about a 25-30 minute drive from downtown Redmond outside rush hour, and a Sunday night show makes the trip and parking easier than a weeknight. Details and tickets at [climatepledgearena.com](https://www.climatepledgearena.com/).
