Gearhouse's **Paddle Club** heads out to **Rattlesnake Lake** on **Saturday, Sept. 5**, a late-morning session (9:30 a.m.-2:30 p.m.) for a relaxed paddle with mountain views instead of a city skyline. Rattlesnake Lake sits right at the base of Rattlesnake Ledge, so it's a genuinely scenic spot — clear, calm water ringed by evergreens, easy for beginners on a board or in a kayak.

Since it's out past North Bend on I-90 rather than into Seattle, this is the easy kind of Gearhouse trip for readers here: drive straight there (about 30-35 minutes from downtown Redmond), no need to loop through the city first. Boards are typically available for members who'd rather not haul their own.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) for the meet spot and whether to bring your own gear. If you want to make a day of it, the Rattlesnake Ledge trailhead is right there for a short hike afterward.
