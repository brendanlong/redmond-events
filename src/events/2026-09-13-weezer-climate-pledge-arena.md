**Weezer** brings **The Gathering Tour** to Seattle on **Sunday, September 13, at 7 p.m.**, with **The Shins** and **Silversun Pickups** rounding out a three-band bill that's basically a greatest-hits lineup for anyone who grew up on 2000s alt/indie radio. Weezer is about as close to the platonic ideal of "nerd-rock" as a band gets — thick glasses, Blue Album singalongs, and a catalog deep enough that the setlist alone is worth the trip. The Shins add wistful indie-pop warmth, and Silversun Pickups bring the fuzzed-out shoegaze-adjacent noise.

It's a Sunday night arena show at Climate Pledge, so the usual Seattle math applies: a ~30-minute drive and real parking hassle, but this is a strong enough bill (and a big enough draw for readers who liked past picks like Beabadoobee or Beck) to be worth the trip in from Redmond. Doors are typically 90 minutes before the show.

Tickets start around $76 via [Ticketmaster](https://climatepledgearena.com/event/weezer-26/); expect this one to move.
