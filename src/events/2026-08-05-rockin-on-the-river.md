The city's free summer concert series is back for three Wednesday evenings on the **Great Lawn**, the grassy stretch between City Hall and the Redmond Senior & Community Center — about as walkable as this newsletter gets. Each night starts at **6 p.m.** and runs rain or shine, with food trucks on-site so you can grab dinner and just set up a blanket.

The lineup is all tribute/cover acts, which fits the low-key, bring-a-lawn-chair vibe of the series:

- **Aug. 12** — **Laurel Canyon Legacy**, playing '60s/'70s folk-rock favorites (Eagles, CSNY-era stuff).
- **Aug. 19** — **Prom Date MixTape**, covering '80s new wave, pop, and rock.
- **Aug. 26** — **Chicago Transit Experience**, a horns-and-harmonies tribute to Chicago — rescheduled from its original Aug. 5 date due to wildfire-smoke air quality concerns.

None of it requires advance planning — just show up. If you're only going to make one, Aug. 12's folk-rock set is probably the widest crowd-pleaser, but honestly any of the three is a good excuse for a short walk and an outdoor evening. Thanks to sponsor Emerald Heights for keeping it free.
