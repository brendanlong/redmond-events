Gearhouse has penciled in a **Thursday-night ski trip to Summit Central at Snoqualmie** on **December 17**, a few hours of turns under the lights after work. All levels are welcome, but there's no instruction included, so ski or ride within your ability (or book a lesson with the resort yourself).

For Redmond readers this is about as easy as weeknight skiing gets. Summit Central is a straight shot up I-90, about **50 minutes from downtown Redmond**, and you're driving east while rush hour heads the other way. The Gearhouse van leaves the Capitol Hill café at 4:45 p.m. and reaches the slopes around **6 p.m.**, which is the time listed above. Driving yourself, you can meet the group there and leave whenever you like. The van rallies to head home at 9 p.m.

Lift tickets, food and lessons aren't included. Gear can be reserved from the [Gearhouse rental library](https://rental.joingearhouse.com/), but it has to be picked up in Capitol Hill before 4:45, which mostly makes sense for van riders. Everyone else will want their own gear or a rental at the pass.

The big caveat: **this is a placeholder date.** Night skiing depends on snow, and early-season dates stay tentative until Summit Central opens its night operations. Rain alone won't cancel it, but low snow or an unsafe or closed pass will. Gearhouse makes the call by 8 p.m. the night before, and the trip needs a five-member minimum. RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/).
