Kirkland's inaugural **Umbrella Fringe Festival**, run by Kirkland Arts Center, spreads independent theater, dance, comedy, and music across downtown Kirkland from **Friday, September 25 through Sunday, September 27** — Kirkland Arts Center itself, Vision Music, Kirkland Urban, the Kirkland Woman's Club, Aegis Waterfront, and (on Sunday) The Village at Totem Lake.

It's a genuine grab-bag: sketch comedy, circus sideshow, Indian classical and Odissi dance, jazz, bossa nova, spoken word, and more, mostly running as short free sets you can drop in and out of. Friday evening is a smaller kickoff (a handful of shows from 6:30–9pm, including one ticketed pop-up at Kirkland Performance Center); Saturday is the big day with dozens of sets across six venues from noon into the evening; Sunday wraps up with a free afternoon block at The Village at Totem Lake.

This is exactly the kind of novel, only-happens-once-a-year cultural event that's worth the drive even without a specific act in mind — a real match for "festivals & cultural celebrations," and the free/drop-in format makes it low-commitment. A 15–20 minute drive to downtown Kirkland. Full lineup and set times at [kirklandartscenter.org/kirkland-fringe-festival](https://kirklandartscenter.org/kirkland-fringe-festival/).
