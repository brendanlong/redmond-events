Seattle Center's **Festál** series turns "All Things Italian" into a full weekend, **Saturday, September 26 through Sunday, September 27**: cooking demos, live music and dance, a classic car show, a dog show, and the festival's famous **Grape Stomp**, where anyone can hop in a barrel and crush grapes the old-fashioned way. Started in 1988, it's grown into the largest Italian-American event in the Northwest — roughly 25,000 people come through over the weekend — so expect a real crowd, not a quiet stroll.

It's exactly the kind of cultural celebration this newsletter likes flagging even without a direct connection to Italian heritage — free, family-friendly, and genuinely festive, in the same spirit as the Ganesh Festival or a Holi celebration. Saturday runs 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., Sunday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., spread across the Armory Food & Event Hall and Fisher Pavilion.

Seattle Center is about a **30-minute drive** from downtown Redmond via 520 — the usual traffic-and-parking caveat applies, but admission itself is free, so the only real cost is your time and whatever you spend on food and wine once inside.
