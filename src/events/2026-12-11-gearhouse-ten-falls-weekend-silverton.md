December is when the waterfalls at Oregon's **Silver Falls State Park** run fullest, and Gearhouse is renting a big house near Silverton for the weekend of **December 11–13** to see them. The centerpiece is Saturday's **Trail of Ten Falls**, a 7.2-mile loop with about 800 feet of elevation change that passes ten waterfalls. You walk *behind* four of them, including 177-foot South Falls. Connector trails let anyone cut the loop short and meet the group back at the lodge.

Skipping the trail is fine too. Frank Lloyd Wright's **Gordon House**, his only Oregon home, runs winter tours Friday through Sunday at noon, 1 and 2 p.m. ($20, 15 people per tour, book ahead). Downtown Silverton has murals, cafés, Silver Falls Brewery and a Christmas market, and the Oregon Garden Resort next door has a spa and a fireside lounge. Like other Gearhouse overnights, it's run cooperatively: meals are cooked together, rides are shared, and everyone helps clean up before checking out at 10 a.m. Sunday.

**Logistics:** Silverton is about **four hours south of Redmond** on I-5, more with Friday traffic. Most people will leave after work Friday, have a simple dinner at the house, and drive home Sunday afternoon. The calendar entries above are rough blocks (Friday-evening arrival, Saturday trail day, Sunday checkout), and the exact house address goes to people who sign up. A **$50 deposit** holds a spot. Your lodging share depends on your membership tier and where you sleep, from a sofa or air mattress up to a private queen room. With ten people that comes to about $91–206 per person, and the price drops as more join. Gas, groceries, the $12 out-of-state day-use parking fee and any tours are extra.

The trip needs ten members to run, the house holds 13, and there's an unlimited waitlist. Pack for December in Oregon: rain, mud and possibly ice. Sign up and fill out the intake survey on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/).
