Two-thirds of the Fugees are reuniting: **Ms. Lauryn Hill and Wyclef Jean** are touring together to mark the 30th anniversary of **The Score**, the 1996 album that turned "Killing Me Softly" and "Ready or Not" into inescapable classics and is still one of the most acclaimed hip-hop/R&B records ever made. The pair has also hinted at new music together, so this isn't purely a nostalgia lap.

It's a genuine arena show — one night, **Tuesday, November 10, at Climate Pledge Arena** — with the kind of cultural weight that's hard to overstate: Hill's solo work (*The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill*) and Wyclef's catalog both loom large over a couple of generations of hip-hop and R&B, and reunion shows like this don't come around often.

Climate Pledge Arena is about a **30-minute drive from downtown Redmond** into Seattle Center, with the usual weeknight parking and traffic hassle that comes with any arena show in the city — but a once-in-a-while reunion tour built around a genuinely canonical album is exactly the kind of thing worth planning the trip around.

Tickets and details at [climatepledgearena.com](https://www.climatepledgearena.com/).
