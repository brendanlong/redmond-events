**Saturday, September 26** is National Public Lands Day, one of the handful of days each year when you don't need a **Discover Pass** to park at Washington State Parks or other state-managed land (DNR and WDFW sites too). Normally that's $10 for a day pass or $30 for an annual one, so it's a free excuse to get out on a trail.

For readers near downtown Redmond, the closest options are all an easy drive: **Lake Sammamish State Park** (about 15 minutes), or the **Tiger Mountain/Squak Mountain trailheads** and **Snoqualmie Point Park** a bit farther out toward the pass — no pass required at any of them this one Saturday. Note the free day doesn't cover overnight stays or facility rentals, just day-use parking.

Details at [discoverpass.wa.gov](https://discoverpass.wa.gov/about-pass/free-ways-visit/free-parking-days).
