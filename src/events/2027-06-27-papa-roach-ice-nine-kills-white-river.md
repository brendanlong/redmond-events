Papa Roach's "Rise of the Roach" tour stops at White River Amphitheatre on **Sunday, June 27, 2027**, with **Ice Nine Kills** and Ninth Key in support. Ice Nine Kills, the horror-movie-obsessed metalcore band, is a name that's come up with these readers before, which is what makes this one worth putting on a calendar a long way out.

White River is in Auburn, roughly **a 45-minute to hour drive** from Redmond depending on I-405 and the Renton stretch, so this is a real outing rather than a quick trip, and it's an outdoor summer-night show. EverOut flagged the on-sale in its Ticket Alert column; check [Ticketmaster/Live Nation](https://www.livenation.com/) for the on-sale and exact door times (the 6 p.m. start here is an estimate).
