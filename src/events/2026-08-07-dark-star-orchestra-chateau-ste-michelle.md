For anyone who wishes they'd caught the Grateful Dead in their prime: **Dark Star Orchestra** re-creates entire Dead setlists from specific historical shows, note-for-note improvisation and all, rather than just playing greatest hits. They bring that to Chateau Ste. Michelle's outdoor amphitheater on **August 7th**.

It's a genuinely different format from a normal tribute act — the band picks an actual date from the Dead's touring history and performs that exact setlist, so longtime Deadheads treat going as a kind of scavenger hunt (guessing which show it is from the first few songs). You don't need to know any of that to enjoy a long, jammy summer night on the lawn, but it adds a layer for anyone who does.

Showtime is **6 p.m.**, with doors about an hour earlier. The venue is in Woodinville, roughly **15 minutes from downtown Redmond** via Redmond–Woodinville Road, with offsite shuttle parking available. Tickets through Ticketmaster; the [full summer lineup](https://www.ste-michelle.com/concerts/summer-concerts) is worth a browse for what else is on the calendar.
