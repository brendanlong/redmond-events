A Creedence Clearwater Revival night in downtown Kirkland: **Revisiting Creedence** is fronted by Dan McGuinness (vocals) and Kurt Griffey (lead guitar), who spent years touring with CCR alumni Stu Cook and Doug "Cosmo" Clifford in Creedence Clearwater Revisited. Expect the hits — "Proud Mary," "Bad Moon Rising," "Fortunate Son," "Green River" — played by people who learned them from the source.

It's **Thursday, November 12, 7–9 p.m.** at the Kirkland Performance Center, about a 20-minute drive from downtown Redmond, in a walkable waterfront-adjacent stretch with plenty of dinner options beforehand. Tickets run $56–$78 through the [KPC site](https://www.kpcenter.org/). A solid, low-hassle alternative to heading into Seattle for a night of classic rock.
