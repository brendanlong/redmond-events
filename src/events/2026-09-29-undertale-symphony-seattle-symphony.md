About as nerdy as a symphony program gets: the **Seattle Symphony performs UNDERTALE: Determination Symphony**, a full orchestral concert built around the beloved indie RPG's soundtrack — newly arranged and performed live, with gameplay footage projected above the stage. It's less a traditional concert than an immersive walk back through the game's story and music, for anyone who's put real hours into Toby Fox's genre-bending 2015 hit (or just loves a good chiptune-turned-orchestral arrangement).

There are two chances to catch it: **Tuesday, September 29, and Tuesday, October 6**, both at Benaroya Hall. Same program either night, so pick whichever date works.

This is squarely in the "nerd-rock" wheelhouse — the kind of gaming-meets-live-orchestra crossover that sits next to a Machinae Supremacy or I Fight Dragons show on the interest list, just with a full symphony instead of a band. Benaroya Hall is downtown Seattle, about a **30-minute drive from Redmond**; a weeknight show means the usual commute-and-parking tradeoff, but this is a genuinely rare pairing of game and orchestra. Tickets are reportedly moving fast for both dates.

Tickets at [seattlesymphony.org](https://www.seattlesymphony.org/benaroyahall).
