Marymoor Park is hosting its first-ever **Smoke on the Sound**, a new KCBS-sanctioned barbecue competition, over the weekend of **September 26–27**. Public tasting hours run 11am–4pm both days: competing teams (judged under Kansas City Barbeque Society rules) serve up samples while you wander with a beer from the beer garden and listen to live music.

This is exactly the kind of food event that clears the bar without needing much else going for it — a walkable new festival with a straightforward, fun premise (eat barbecue, vote with your taste buds) rather than something you have to be a die-hard fan of anything to enjoy. Pre-sale tasting-token bundles are the better deal if you know you're going; single tokens are available at the gate too.

Full details and pre-sale tokens at [smokeonthesound.com](https://www.smokeonthesound.com/).
