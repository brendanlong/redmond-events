If you've got a kid who loses their mind over anything with big tires, **Big Truck Day** is the event: from **noon to 3 p.m. on Friday, Aug. 14**, Farrel-McWhirter Farm Park fills up with dump trucks, tractors, and construction equipment that kids can climb into and sit behind the wheel of. There's also a tractor-pulled wagon ride around the park and a goody bag for participants. It's free, but [pre-registration is encouraged](https://www.redmond.gov/165/Parks-Recreation) since spots for the truck-climbing tend to fill up — and be ready for engine noise if your kid's sensitive to that sort of thing.

Farrel-McWhirter is on the north side of Redmond, about a 10-minute drive from downtown (not walkable, but an easy short hop). It's also a working farm park with animals and trails, so it's worth lingering afterward if the trucks aren't enough excitement for one afternoon.
