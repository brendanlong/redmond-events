Humorist **David Sedaris** returns to Benaroya Hall's Taper Auditorium — reportedly his favorite American venue — on **Sunday, November 15**, touring his latest essay collection, *The Land and Its People*. It's not a straight reading: Sedaris reads new work, riffs through book recommendations, does a live Q&A with the audience, and personally signs every book in the building afterward. He's one of the sharpest, funniest writers working, and this is his one-night-only Seattle stop.

It's a Sunday evening, about a 30-minute drive downtown from Redmond via SR 520, with the usual weekend-into-Seattle parking to plan around — worth it for a rare one-night appearance rather than a recurring show.

Tickets and details at [benaroyahall.org](https://www.benaroyahall.org/).
