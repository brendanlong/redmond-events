**Beck**'s Ride Lonesome Tour has moved: the show originally booked for Chateau Ste. Michelle on September 18 got rescheduled to **Sunday, November 8, at 7 p.m. at Benaroya Hall** in downtown Seattle. Same case as before for anyone whose taste runs toward the weird, eclectic, genre-hopping end of rock — the guy who made "Loser" and "Where It's At" and then turned around and made a folk-country breakup album with *Sea Change* — this is still about as close to a can't-miss as the fall concert calendar gets. Expect a set pulling from across a three-decade catalog rather than a single-genre greatest-hits run.

The venue swap trades the Woodinville wine-country lawn for Benaroya's indoor concert hall downtown — a roughly 30-minute drive from Redmond (or a bus/light rail combo if you'd rather skip parking), with the usual Seattle weeknight traffic to plan around. Indoors also means no weather gamble this time.

Tickets and details at [benaroyahall.org](https://www.benaroyahall.org/).
