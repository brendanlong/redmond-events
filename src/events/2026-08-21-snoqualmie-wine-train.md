A genuinely novel date-night idea: the **Northwest Railway Museum** in Snoqualmie runs a **Wine Train Excursion** on select Friday evenings — a 2.5-3 hour round-trip train ride with wine tastings at both ends. Your ticket gets a souvenir wine glass, eight tasting tokens (redeemable for 1-oz pours from local wineries at the Snoqualmie Depot and the museum's Train Shed Exhibit Hall), and hors d'oeuvres from local businesses. It's 21+, and tickets are **$99**.

There are three dates on the books: **August 21, September 18, and October 30**, all departing at **5 p.m.** Snoqualmie is about a **25-30 minute drive** east from downtown Redmond via I-90 — an easy evening trip, and a good excuse to see the valley from the rails instead of the highway. The **August 21** date also happens to fall during **Snoqualmie Days**, the town's summer festival, so if you're up for making a full evening of it, the timing lines up.

Tickets are non-refundable, so be sure of the date before you book. Full details and tickets at [trainmuseum.org](https://trainmuseum.org/).
