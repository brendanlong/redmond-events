Gearhouse is doing a "Late Shift" version of one of Washington's most popular trails: **Lake 22**, on **Saturday, August 1**, heading out in the afternoon specifically to dodge the notoriously packed weekend-morning crowds and parking scrum. It's a moderate 5.4-mile round trip with about 1,350 feet of gain, climbing through old-growth rainforest along a rushing creek to a big alpine lake sitting right under Mount Pilchuck — 3 to 4 hours at a casual, social pace.

Not a morning person's problem this time: the whole point of the later start is a calmer trail and an actual shot at parking. It's a good one for anyone newer to Gearhouse hikes or just wanting a proper Washington classic without the sunrise alarm.

The **time listed above is when to meet the group at the Lake 22 trailhead** — the carpool leaves Gearhouse's Capitol Hill shop at **1:30 p.m.**, and it's about a 1.5-hour drive up the Mountain Loop Highway past Granite Falls, so plan to be at the trailhead around **3 p.m.**. That's far enough out that driving straight there from the Eastside beats the trip into Seattle first. A Northwest Forest Pass (or America the Beautiful Pass) is required to park.

Non-Summit members need to arrange their own transportation to and from the trailhead. RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) and jump in the Slack channel to coordinate a carpool if you're driving yourself.
