New Orleans horn player **Trombone Shorty** brings his band **Orleans Avenue** to Marymoor Park on **Wednesday, August 19**, doors at 4 p.m., show at 5:30 p.m. **St. Paul and the Broken Bones** — a soul/R&B outfit out of Alabama with a genuinely electric frontman — opens. This is a horns-and-groove kind of double bill, more brass-band-meets-funk than anything else on the Marymoor calendar this summer, and both acts are known for high-energy live shows rather than just playing the album straight.

Another easy weeknight yes given the walk-distance venue — get there, catch two bands built around live-show energy, walk home after.
