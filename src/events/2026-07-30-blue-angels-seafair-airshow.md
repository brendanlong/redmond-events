The **Blue Angels** are back over Lake Washington for **Seafair weekend**, July 30 through August 2. The Navy's flight demo squadron practices Thursday (two runs, 11 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.) before the full air show flies Friday through Sunday, with the Blue Angels' set at **3:30 p.m.** each day. It's genuinely one of the more spectacular free things to watch in the region — F/A-18s doing tight formation passes low over the water, loud enough to feel in your chest.

**Friday admission is free**, and even on the ticketed days, plenty of people watch for free from public shoreline and parks around Lake Washington rather than paying for the Stan Sayres grandstand seats — Seward Park, Genesee Park, and the I-90 floating bridge area are all popular unofficial viewing spots. If you want a guaranteed seat and don't mind paying, premium seating is sold separately, and viewing cruises depart from Kirkland and Lake Union if you'd rather watch from the water. The Museum of Flight also runs a "Jet Blast Bash" alongside it with food vendors and family workshops, for a lower-key add-on.

It's a genuine trip into Seattle — Stan Sayres Park is on the south end of Lake Washington, expect 30-40 minutes each way and heavy Seafair traffic/parking around showtime — but the spectacle is the kind of once-a-year thing worth the hassle, especially for the free Friday show. Details at [seafair.org](https://seafair.org/).
