Mundo Catrina's **Kirkland Catrina Walk** fills Marina Park on the Kirkland waterfront on **Saturday, October 31, from 11 a.m. to 8:30 p.m.** It's a Día de Muertos celebration with live music, cultural performances, art, local vendors, family activities, and a Catrina walk through downtown Kirkland. Catrinas are the elegant skeleton figures of Mexican tradition, so people dress up in the full face paint and finery.

It's free and runs all day, which makes it an easy Halloween-day outing, about a 20-minute drive from downtown Redmond. If you liked Redmond's Holi celebration, this is the same kind of colorful, community-driven festival. Parking in downtown Kirkland is tight, so consider the municipal garage under the library. Related: the City of Kirkland hosts a free Día de los Muertos event at the Peter Kirk Community Center on October 24, 2–4 p.m. More at [Explore Kirkland](https://www.explorekirkland.com/events/).
