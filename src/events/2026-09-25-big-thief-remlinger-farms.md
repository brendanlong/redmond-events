**Big Thief** — one of the most acclaimed indie/folk bands working right now — is playing an unusually intimate show for a band their size: an outdoor set at **Remlinger Farms** in Carnation on **Friday, September 25** (doors 4:30pm, show at 6pm), rather than a city venue.

If you're into the indie side of "live music, broad taste," this is a genuinely notable booking — a farm-venue show trades arena scale for a smaller, more atmospheric setting, and Adrianne Lenker's songwriting is the kind of thing that rewards actually paying attention. It's not cheap (tickets start around $141), but it's a rare booking for the area.

It's about a **20–25 minute drive out via SR-203**, away from Seattle traffic rather than into it — genuinely easier than most "big show" nights. Tickets via [remlingerfarms.com](https://remlingerfarms.com/big-thief/).
