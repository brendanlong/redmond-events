**Hermanos Gutiérrez** close out the Chateau Ste. Michelle season on **Sunday, September 27** — a Swiss-Ecuadorian brother duo whose instrumental guitar music (think reverb-soaked, desert-noir grooves, no vocals needed) has quietly become one of the more distinctive things happening in indie music the last few years. It's a mellower, more atmospheric booking than most of the summer's lineup — good for a laid-back Sunday evening rather than a singalong. **Y La Bamba** opens.

Gates open around **5:30 p.m.** for the **7 p.m.** start. Bring a blanket or low chairs for the lawn. Woodinville is a **15-20 minute drive** from downtown Redmond — easy enough for a Sunday evening, and a nice way to close out the summer concert season.

Tickets and full details at [ste-michelle.com](https://www.ste-michelle.com/concerts/summer-concerts), via Ticketmaster.
