Redmond's own way to mark the longest night: **Solstice at the Farm** ("Return of the Light") is back at Farrel-McWhirter Park on the actual solstice, **Monday, December 21, from 5 to 8 p.m.**

The evening centers on a **luminary trail through the park's woods** — a short, glowing walk in the dark — plus **live music inside the 1930s barn**, a craft room, and a hot drink and dessert included with admission. It's low-key and very local, the kind of thing that feels like a small-town tradition rather than a big production. If you're going to the rationalist Secular Solstice in Seattle two nights earlier, this is a nice, gentler bookend; if you're not, it's the easy, close-to-home way to do something seasonal that isn't shopping.

Farrel-McWhirter is about a 10-minute drive east of downtown Redmond on Redmond Road (the same park that hosts the fall Harvest Festival), with free parking at the park. It's outdoors in December, so dress for cold and probably rain, and bring footwear that can handle a muddy trail.

Admission is **$15–$18** (listed for ages 9 and up; kids under 8 get in free), and registration is through the city's recreation portal — [sign up here](https://app.amilia.com/store/en/city-of-redmond/shop/activities/7184331).
