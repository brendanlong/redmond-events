Every night from **November 27 through December 24**, downtown Bellevue puts on **Snowflake Lane**, a short free holiday parade along **Bellevue Way NE between NE 4th and NE 8th Streets**, outside Bellevue Square and Lincoln Square. It starts at **7 p.m. sharp** and has lighted floats, toy-soldier drummers, dancers, holiday music and "snow" falling from the buildings. It's a little over the top in a way kids and the kid-at-heart love. The listing above is opening night (the day after Thanksgiving), but it runs every evening through Christmas Eve.

It's a nice low-commitment outing for Redmond readers. Downtown Bellevue is about a **15-minute drive**, and The Bellevue Collection's garages are free, so you can park, grab a spot along the curb, and pair the parade with dinner or holiday shopping. Even easier, the **2 Line** runs from Downtown Redmond station to Bellevue Downtown station, a short walk from the route, so you can skip holiday parking entirely.

The organizers don't list a run time, but the parade is short, so it fits on a school night. Weekend nights and the days before Christmas draw the biggest crowds, and a weeknight early in the season is the relaxed choice. Dress for standing around in the cold and wet. If you're making a night of Eastside lights, Garden d'Lights at the Bellevue Botanical Garden is about a 10-minute drive away. Details and a parade-route map are on the [Bellevue Collection's Snowflake Lane page](https://bellevuecollection.com/snowflakelane/).
