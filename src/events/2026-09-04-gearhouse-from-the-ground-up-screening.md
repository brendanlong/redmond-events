A one-off screening for the outdoors crowd: Gearhouse is showing **"From the Ground Up: The Andean Women Remaking the Mountaineering World,"** a documentary following indigenous Peruvian mountaineers Yudith Mamani and Lixayda Vásquez into Peru's Cordillera Vilcanota range — tracing their path from indigenous communities to becoming formidable climbers on peaks their ancestors considered holy. It's followed by a **Q&A with the filmmakers**, on **Friday, September 4**.

A nice change of pace from Gearhouse's usual hike/paddle/climb listings — a genuine film screening with the people who made it in the room, rather than a group outing. Good fit if the mountaineering documentary genre or a look at a different climbing culture appeals, on top of the usual club social scene.

Same weeknight-in-Seattle honesty as always: the Gearhouse shop is on Capitol Hill, about a **25–30 minute drive from downtown Redmond**. It's a short evening program (90 minutes), so an easier lift than a full outing if the film itself is the draw.
