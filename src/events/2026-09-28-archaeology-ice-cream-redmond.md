The Snoqualmie Indian Tribe and the City of Redmond team up for **Archaeology and Ice Cream** on **Monday, September 28, from 6:30 to 8 p.m.** at Redmond City Hall — free ice cream alongside a family-friendly, interactive look at how the city and the Tribe jointly protect cultural and natural resources, including the Bear Creek archaeological site. It's a genuinely local, walkable evening that's part community history lesson, part dessert.

Redmond City Hall is right downtown, an easy walk for most readers. Casual and kid-friendly — no registration mentioned, just show up.

Details at [redmond.gov](https://www.redmond.gov/).
