The Friday before Halloween, Redmond Fire and a few community partners turn the parking lot beside the Municipal Campus garage into a trick-or-treat route for the evening: decorated trunks, candy, games, and plenty of costumes, from **5:00 to 7:30 p.m. on Friday, October 30**.

It's a good option if you'd rather skip the door-to-door loop or want to start the night early with a younger crowd: it's free, open to all ages, and the lot sits right behind City Hall in downtown Redmond, so it's an easy walk or a very short drive from nearly everywhere in the core. Parking nearby will fill up, so walking is the better bet.

The City's announcement points to a Facebook RSVP page for details; there's nothing to buy, and wearing a costume is encouraged. More Halloween-season happenings are collected on the [City of Redmond site](https://www.redmond.gov/).
