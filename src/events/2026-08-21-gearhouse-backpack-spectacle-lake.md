A bigger outing for the weekend of **August 21-23**: a member-organized **backpacking trip to Spectacle Lake**, out past Cle Elum in the Alpine Lakes Wilderness. This one's a step up from a casual day hike — 22 miles round-trip with about 1,500 feet of gain via the Pete Lake Trailhead — so it's aimed at people who are already comfortable carrying an overnight pack, not a first backpacking trip.

The plan is loose: head out Friday afternoon and grab a campsite near Cle Elum/Roslyn, drive to the trailhead and hike in to Spectacle Lake Saturday, then pack up and head home Sunday. Unlike Gearhouse's staff-run trips, this one is **member-led** — you coordinate directly with the organizer rather than joining an official shuttle — so treat the times here as a starting point and confirm specifics when you RSVP. It's about a **2-hour drive** from Redmond out I-90 to the trailhead, easy enough to meet the group there directly on Saturday even if you skip the Friday night camp.

If a proper 22-mile weekend in the Alpine Lakes Wilderness sounds like your kind of long weekend, RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) and connect with the organizer for carpool and campsite details. Bring full overnight gear — this is real backcountry mileage, not a car-camping trip.
