**The Smashing Pumpkins** play Climate Pledge Arena on **Friday, November 6**. If your taste runs to the big, loud, melodic end of 90s alternative — *Siamese Dream*, "Today," "1979," "Tonight, Tonight" — this is the headline rock show of the first week of November. Billy Corgan's band has spent the last few years touring hard on a catalog that holds up, and it tends to run long and heavy on the guitars.

It's a Seattle weeknight-adjacent Friday show, so budget for the usual: roughly 30 minutes from Redmond without traffic, a lot more with it, and paid parking around Seattle Center. The Link/monorail option is worth considering if you'd rather skip the 520 and downtown crawl. Our source listing didn't include door or showtimes, so the 7:30 p.m. start here is an estimate — confirm times and tickets on the [venue's events page](https://climatepledgearena.com/events/).
