Gearhouse is running **weekly Thursday evening rock climbing sessions at Exit 38** on I-90 — outdoor sport climbing on actual stone, with a crew of members ranging from beginners to people sending pumpy overhangs. It's a carpool event that leaves from Capitol Hill, and the access from Redmond is actually pretty clean: jump on I-90 heading east, and you're there in about 35–40 minutes without crossing any bridges.

Exit 38 (near North Bend) is one of the closest accessible outdoor climbing spots to the Seattle metro area — bolted sport routes at all grades, roadside access, and good afternoon sun even as the days shorten into fall. For anyone who's been bouldering indoors and wants to try real rock, this is the natural next step.

The carpool leaves Gearhouse's Capitol Hill shop at **4:30 p.m. every Thursday**, with dates on the calendar now running through **October 22**, and climbs wrap up around **9**. Capitol Hill is the intended assembly point, but the crag is only about 45 minutes east of the shop (and even closer for us) — so for Redmond folks the easy move is to skip the trip into the city and **drive straight to the crag, where the crew rolls in around 5:15** (that's the time listed above). If you'd rather share a ride, you can also head into Seattle to catch the carpool or pick up riders on the way.

For brand-new climbers: show up, tell them you're new, and you'll be paired with someone who can show you the basics. For experienced climbers: RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) so they know to expect you and can size the carpool.
