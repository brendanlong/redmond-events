**Fall Out Boy** is bringing its 2027 **Twilight of the Punks** stadium tour to **T-Mobile Park on Tuesday, August 24, 2027**. The supporting lineup reads like a 2000s Warped Tour reunion: **The Offspring**, **Good Charlotte** and **The Story So Far**. The tour follows "Aces," the band's first new song in three years, and Seattle is the next-to-last North American stop before Portland two nights later.

For readers with broad, singalong-friendly taste (the Marina and Taylor Swift side of the list as much as the louder side), this is an easy pick. You get three bands' worth of hits you'll know every word to, outdoors in late summer. Fall Out Boy is also the first act to book a 2027 concert at the Mariners' ballpark, so it should be a big night.

The catch is the usual Seattle one: it's a **Tuesday night in SoDo**, about **30 minutes from Redmond** without traffic and a lot more at 5 p.m. on a summer weekday. The low-stress route is the **2 Line** from Downtown Redmond, transferring to the 1 Line at International District/Chinatown. Stadium station is a short walk from the gates, and you skip event parking entirely. Show times haven't been announced yet, so the 6 p.m. start above is a placeholder for a typical three-band stadium bill. Check your ticket for doors.

Tickets went on sale November 12 through the [Mariners' concerts page](https://www.mlb.com/mariners/tickets/concerts). Stadium shows with this kind of lineup sell the good floor and lower-bowl seats early.
