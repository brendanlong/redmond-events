**Sarah McLachlan** brings her **Better Broken Tour** to the Chateau Ste. Michelle lawn on **Saturday, August 8**, with **Allison Russell** opening. Gates open around 5:30 p.m. for the 7 p.m. show. McLachlan's a genuine legacy act at this point — Grammy winner, Lilith Fair founder, the voice behind "Angel" and "Building a Mystery" — and Allison Russell (roots/Americana, several Grammy nods of her own) is a strong complement rather than a filler opener.

It's the same winery-lawn format as the other Chateau shows we've covered this summer: bring a blanket, arrive early to stake out lawn space, and expect a relaxed evening rather than a mosh pit. It's a 15-20 minute drive out to Woodinville from downtown Redmond — solidly in the "worth the short drive" tier if either artist is your thing.

Tickets and full details at [ste-michelle.com](https://www.ste-michelle.com/concerts/summer-concerts), via Ticketmaster.
