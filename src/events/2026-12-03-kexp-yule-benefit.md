KEXP's annual **Yule Benefit** is hitting the road this year, and Seattle's stop lands at The Showbox on **Thursday, December 3**: jazz saxophonist **Kamasi Washington** headlining alongside acclaimed indie artist **Bartees Strange**.

This is a strong live-music pairing — Washington is one of the most respected names in jazz right now, and Bartees Strange covers the indie/genre-bending side of "broad taste" well. Benefit shows for KEXP also tend to draw a good crowd and support a station that's a genuine local institution.

It's far enough out to just note for now — check [kexp.org/events](https://www.kexp.org/events/) closer to the date for ticket links and final set times. About a 30-minute drive into the city for a Thursday night show.
