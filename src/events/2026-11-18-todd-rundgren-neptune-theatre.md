**Todd Rundgren** — the studio wizard behind "Hello It's Me," "Bang the Drum All Day," and the prog-pop gem *A Wizard, a True Star*, and producer of Meat Loaf's *Bat Out of Hell* — plays the **Neptune Theatre in the U-District on Wednesday, November 18**. He's famously restless on stage, so a set can swing from blue-eyed soul to synth-heavy oddities to straight-ahead rock, which makes this a good pick for readers who like their nerd-rock with some real musical ambition behind it.

The Neptune is a mid-size, seated historic theater, a much easier Seattle trip than the downtown arenas: about 25 minutes from Redmond via 520 and the Montlake/I-5 corner, with parking in the U-District that's annoying but manageable. It's a weeknight, so factor in the drive home. Our source listing gave no showtime, so the 8 p.m. here is an estimate; check [STG Presents](https://www.stgpresents.org/venue/neptune-theatre) for tickets and doors.
