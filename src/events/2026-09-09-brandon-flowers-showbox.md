**Brandon Flowers**, frontman of The Killers, brings his solo tour — behind his country-inflected new album *Thrasher* — to The Showbox on **Wednesday, September 9** (doors 7pm, show 8pm).

A big-name arena frontman playing an intimate downtown club is exactly the kind of "particularly interesting" booking that clears Seattle's higher bar, and it's a real departure from The Killers' sound if you're curious what a solo, more roots-leaning set looks like from him.

It's a weeknight into the city — about a 30-minute drive with the usual parking hassle — so this is one for readers genuinely excited about the show rather than a casual "why not." Tickets via [showboxpresents.com](https://www.showboxpresents.com/events/detail/1497651).
