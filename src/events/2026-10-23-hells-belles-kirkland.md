For a loud Friday night close to home, **Hell's Belles** bring their all-female AC/DC tribute to Kirkland Performance Center on **October 23 at 8 p.m.** The band has an international following and, per its billing, the endorsement of Angus Young himself. Expect "Highway to Hell," "Back in Black," "Thunderstruck," "T.N.T.," and the rest of the catalog at volume.

Kirkland is about a 20-minute drive from downtown Redmond. This is the fun-over-fame pick of the Eastside's October concert calendar; tickets are via [Explore Kirkland](https://www.explorekirkland.com/events/).
