Gearhouse is turning **5** on **Thursday, Sept. 10**, and the Capitol Hill shop is throwing itself a party: special drinks, gear giveaways, and a raffle for a grand prize of **$1,000 in gear-library credit plus 3 months of Summit membership**. It's the club celebrating five years of being Seattle's "third place" for outdoor types — part coffee shop, part gear library, part meetup hub.

This is exactly the kind of weeknight-into-Seattle trip that usually doesn't clear the bar for readers here, but a once-every-five-years anniversary bash with a four-figure raffle prize is a reasonable exception if you're already a member. If you're not going to make the regular trivia or board game nights, this is the one worth the drive in.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) to save a spot — space is limited for the party.
