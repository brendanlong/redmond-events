The Fray's frontman **Isaac Slade** is doing a solo tour, and it's landing close to home: **Kirkland Performance Center on Thursday, September 10th**. Tickets just went on sale. If The Fray's mid-2000s run (readers already caught them at Chateau Ste. Michelle in July) meant anything to you, this is the same voice behind "How to Save a Life" and "You Found Me," reworked as a stripped-down solo show — likely just Slade, a piano, and the songs, without the full band arrangement.

That format tends to favor the storytelling side of a songwriter over the anthemic side, so expect more between-song context and quieter arrangements than the band's stadium-sized choruses. It's billed as "Songs I Know," which suggests a set pulling from his own catalog and the songs that shaped him rather than a strict Fray hits set.

Kirkland Performance Center is a proper theater — assigned seating, good acoustics — a solid **10–15 minute drive** from downtown Redmond. Shows here typically start around 7:30 p.m. on weeknights (used above as an estimate); confirm the exact time when you grab tickets. Details at [kpcenter.org](https://www.kpcenter.org/).
