For the two-wheeled crowd, Gearhouse is running a casual **Fall Colors Bike Ride** along the
**Burke-Gilman Trail** on **Saturday, October 24** — a flat, car-free cruise along Lake
Washington's shoreline built for golden foliage and conversation rather than speed, with
stops for food, coffee, or drinks wherever the group feels like it.

Gearhouse's crew meets at the Capitol Hill coffee shop at **10 a.m.** and takes the light
rail from Capitol Hill Station to UW Station to skip the hill climb out of the neighborhood,
rolling onto the trail from there around **10:20**. Redmond riders can skip that detour
entirely — it's a straightforward **20–25 minute drive** via SR-520 straight to the U-District,
where you can meet the group right at UW Station as they roll off the train.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/). Bring your own bike and
helmet.
