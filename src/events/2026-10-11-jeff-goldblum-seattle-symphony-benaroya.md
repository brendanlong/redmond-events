Yes, that Jeff Goldblum. For **30 years**, the actor has fronted a real working jazz band — **The Mildred Snitzer Orchestra** — playing contemporary arrangements of jazz and American Songbook standards, and on **Sunday, October 11**, they're joining the **Seattle Symphony** at Benaroya Hall for full orchestral arrangements of the band's tunes, including material from their latest album, *Night Blooms*.

It's the kind of genuinely odd, one-night-only crossover this newsletter tends to flag — a Hollywood A-lister's actual, decades-running jazz combo backed by a full symphony orchestra, in the same vein as the Studio Ghibli and *Undertale* symphony nights already on the calendar this fall. If you like jazz, or you just want to see Jeff Goldblum lean into a piano riff live, this clears the bar on novelty alone.

Showtime is **4 p.m.**, at Benaroya Hall downtown — about a **30-minute drive** from Redmond via 520, with the usual Seattle parking-and-traffic tax to budget for on top of the ticket. Tickets start around **$114** through the Seattle Symphony box office and major resellers.
