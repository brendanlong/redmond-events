For the larch chasers: Gearhouse is heading out to the **Swauk Forest Discovery Trail** near
Blewett Pass on **Saturday, October 10**, for a relaxed, easy hike through forest and open
meadows timed to catch the larches turning gold. It's a short one by Cascades standards —
about 2.5 miles round trip with 660 feet of elevation gain, topping out around 4,530 feet —
with plenty of stops for photos and a trailside lunch.

The trailhead itself has no formal street address — it's a pullout off NF-9716, reached from
Highway 97 about 21 miles south of the US 2/97 junction toward Blewett Pass. Gearhouse's
Capitol Hill crew carpools out together at **7:30 a.m.**; figure roughly **2 hours** of
driving each way from Seattle, putting the group at the trailhead around **9:45 a.m.** —
Redmond hikers can skip the trip into the city and drive straight out via I-90 to meet the
group there instead, at a similar overall drive time.

This is a genuine drive for a short hike, but larch season is a narrow two-week window each
fall, which is exactly the kind of trip worth planning around. RSVP on the
[Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) for carpool coordination.
