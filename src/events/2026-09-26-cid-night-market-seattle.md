Seattle's **Chinatown-International District Night Market** returns to Hing Hay Park for its 19th year on **Saturday, September 26, from 1 to 9 p.m.** — 90+ vendors under the park's red arch, street food, handmade goods, a mahjong pop-up, and live performances including lion dance, taiko drumming, and martial arts demos. It's free, all-ages, and one of the better cultural-festival nights Seattle puts on all year.

It's the kind of event worth the trip into the city for: genuinely novel, not something you can get closer to home, and a full afternoon-into-evening of things to see and eat rather than a quick stop. It's about a 30-minute drive from Redmond via I-90 — this is a Saturday, so parking in the CID will be tighter than a weeknight; the light rail (International District/Chinatown station) is a solid alternative if you'd rather skip it.

Details at [seattlechinatownid.com](https://www.seattlechinatownid.com/experiences/c-id-night-market).
