**Mystery Science Theater 3000** is taking its live show back on the road, and the "Returnen-ing" tour stops at Seattle's historic **Moore Theatre** on **Thursday, March 25, 2027**.

If you grew up on Joel, Mike, Crow and Tom Servo heckling terrible movies, the live version is exactly that, in a room full of people who also know every running joke: a cast riffs along to a famously bad film on the big screen, with puppets, sketches, and songs worked in around it. It's about as millennial-nerd as live entertainment gets, and the shared-audience laughter makes it a genuinely different experience from watching at home.

The Moore is in Belltown at 2nd Ave and Virginia St, about a 25–30 minute drive from downtown Redmond via SR 520 or I-90 — but on a Thursday, heading in around evening rush hour is the real cost, and downtown parking runs $15–$25 at nearby garages. Light rail is a reasonable alternative; Westlake station is about a 5-minute walk away. Showtime hasn't been widely posted yet, so check the listing; Moore shows typically start around 7:30 p.m.

Tickets through [STG Presents](https://www.stgpresents.org/) (the Moore's operator).
