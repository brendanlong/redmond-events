For the Alestorm/Amaranthe crowd: American metal band **Trivium** bring their **Crown in the Grave World Tour** to the Paramount Theatre on **Wednesday, December 9th**. Tickets just went on sale. Trivium have been a mainstay of the modern metal scene for two decades — technical, riff-heavy metalcore with the kind of soaring, melodic choruses that put them closer to power metal than to the screamo end of the genre, which lines up well with a taste for Alestorm and Amaranthe.

The Paramount is one of Seattle's best rooms for a heavier show — a real theater with a pit up front and balcony seating if you'd rather headbang sitting down — and a December date means this is one to lock in early, since it's a weeknight downtown in the thick of holiday-traffic season.

It's a real trip into the city (budget for parking or take the light rail to Westlake, a couple blocks' walk), but for a band this squarely in the reader wheelhouse, it clears the bar. Tickets and full details at [stgpresents.org](https://www.stgpresents.org/paramount).
