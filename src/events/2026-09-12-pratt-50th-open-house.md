Pratt Fine Arts Center turns **50** this year, and it's throwing a free, all-ages open house on **Saturday, September 12, from 5 to 8 p.m.** — timed to overlap with the **Jackson Street Jazz Festival**, so there's live jazz on two outdoor stages alongside a live bronze pour, steamroller printmaking demos, hands-on artmaking for all ages, an artist and makers market, food trucks, and a beer and wine garden spread across the campus and Pratt Park.

It's a genuinely novel one-off — a milestone anniversary crossed with a free neighborhood arts festival, the kind of thing worth a Seattle trip even without a personal connection to the studio. It's also the same night as the Stranger Genius Awards downtown, so anyone making a night of it in Seattle could plausibly do both (Pratt runs 5-8 p.m., well ahead of the Genius Awards' evening program).

No RSVP required — just show up. More at [pratt.org](https://www.pratt.org/).
