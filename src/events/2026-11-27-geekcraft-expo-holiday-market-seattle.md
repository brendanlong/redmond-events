If your gift list runs to D20s, Pokémon-themed pottery and Tolkien cross-stitch, the **GeekCraft Expo Holiday Market** is the one-stop shop. It's a curated market where local makers sell only handmade, geek-themed goods: fandom art prints, enamel pins, crocheted creatures, dice bags, nerdy jewelry and the like. It fills **Hangar 30 at Magnuson Park** on Thanksgiving weekend, and the exhibitor list is sold out, so expect a full hall.

There are three ways in. **Friday, November 27, 6–9 p.m.** is a ticketed "pre-shop" capped at 500 people, about $15.75, for first pick and smaller crowds. **Saturday, November 28 (10 a.m.–5 p.m.)** and **Sunday, November 29 (10 a.m.–4 p.m.)** are the main days. Admission is a nominal couple of dollars, and kids 12 and under get in free.

It's in Seattle, but this is the easy kind of Seattle trip. Magnuson Park is at Sand Point in northeast Seattle, about **25 minutes from Redmond across SR 520** on a holiday-weekend Saturday, and there's free parking at the park. It's the same venue as the Renegade Craft Fair the weekend before, if you want to compare. Pair it with a walk along the Magnuson Park shoreline, or come back east in time for Snowflake Lane in Bellevue that evening. Advance tickets are on the [GeekCraft Expo site](https://www.geekcraftexpo.com/).
