The Sunday after Thanksgiving, Gearhouse is heading to **Von Sauna**, a wood-fired floating sauna in the marina at Carillon Point in Kirkland. The plan is rounds of heat followed by quick plunges into Lake Washington, then warming back up as the light fades over the water, from **1:30 to 5 p.m. on Sunday, November 29**.

This one is easy for Redmond readers: Carillon Point is on the Kirkland waterfront, roughly **a 20-minute drive** from downtown Redmond, so there's no need to go anywhere near Seattle. First-timers are covered, since the hosts walk newcomers through how sauna rounds work. It's a good cure for a long holiday weekend. Bring a swimsuit and towel, and RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/).
