Not your usual Seattle concert bill: **RuPaul** — yes, the Emmy-winning host of *RuPaul's Drag Race* — is playing a live outdoor **DJ set** at **Brick Park** in Pioneer Square on **Saturday, August 15**. Beyond the TV career, RuPaul is a working recording artist and DJ, with a dance-music catalog that includes "Supermodel (You Better Work)" and "Call Me Mother"; the set leans into that side of the résumé rather than drag-show theatrics. Seattle house-scene veteran **Wesley Holmes** and local artist **JusMoni** (R&B/hip-hop/soul/spoken word) round out the lineup.

This is squarely a "particularly interesting" pick for the Seattle tier — a genuine pop-culture icon doing a rare outdoor DJ show, not just another touring band. Brick Park is an open-air lot in historic Pioneer Square, a roughly 20-minute drive from downtown Redmond without a bridge crossing, though parking in the neighborhood on a Saturday night takes some planning (garages nearby, or rideshare in).

Doors and start are **7 p.m.**, with a midnight curfew — a full night out rather than a quick show. It's a **21+** event, tickets start around **$55.71** via [Tixr](https://www.tixr.com/groups/rbfestivals/events/rupaul-198497), presented by Relentless Beats and Hometeam.
