Two founding members of Seattle's own **Heart**, bassist Steve Fossen and drummer Michael Derosier, front **Heart by Heart** at Kirkland Performance Center on **Friday, October 9, at 8 p.m.** Expect the hits ("Barracuda," "Crazy on You," "Magic Man," "Straight On") plus deep cuts, and a special run through songs from *Dreamboat Annie*, which turns 50 this year.

It's a local-legend, sing-along kind of night in an intimate theater, about a 20-minute drive from downtown Redmond. Tickets are comparatively cheap for a show with this much hometown history; find them via [Explore Kirkland](https://www.explorekirkland.com/events/).
