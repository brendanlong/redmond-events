An easy after-work outing for the hiking crowd: Gearhouse is leading a relaxed evening hike
to **Franklin Falls** on **Thursday, October 8**, gathering at the trailhead at **4:30 p.m.**
It's a short one — about 2 miles round trip with 400 feet of elevation gain — following the
forest trail at a conversational pace to catch the fall colors while there's still light,
with time at the waterfall itself before heading back as the sun sets around 6:30.

The trailhead sits off **I-90 exit 47** near North Bend, which makes this one genuinely easy
for Redmond: it's about a **35–40 minute drive east** on I-90, in the opposite direction from
the Seattle-bound Gearhouse crowd's usual Capitol Hill commute. Bring a headlamp for the
walk back, since part of the return will be after dark.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) — a Northwest Forest Pass
(or America the Beautiful Pass) is required to park at the trailhead.
