For a real getaway: **Gearhouse** has reserved two campsites for a **Deception Pass Campout Weekend**, Friday through Sunday, **August 28-30**. It's deliberately unstructured — camping, hiking, beach time, and campfires around Deception Pass and Whidbey Island, with members free to join group hikes (Goose Rock is the signature one) or just hang out at camp. It's the kind of trip where you can go as hard or as easy as you want.

Tickets run **$30-$65** depending on tier, which mainly covers the shared campsite reservations; food isn't included, so plan to bring your own or coordinate a shared meal with the group. Summit members get complimentary Gearhouse transportation leaving Capitol Hill Friday afternoon, but honestly, the easier move for our readers is to skip that entirely and **drive straight out to Deception Pass** — about a **2-hour drive** from Redmond via I-5, versus first crossing into Seattle to catch a shuttle that's headed the same direction you'd be driving anyway.

Space is limited (Gearhouse has 12 spots across two campsites), and it's a paid RSVP, so don't wait if a weekend of camping and beachcombing on Whidbey Island sounds good. Bring your own tent, sleeping bag, and pad — Gearhouse gear rental is available but fills up fast for campout weekends, so reserve early if you need it.

RSVP and full ticket tiers on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/).
