A fall-night twist on a farm classic: Gearhouse is heading to **Bob's Corn & Pumpkin Farm** in Snohomish on **Thursday, October 22** to tackle their 12-acre corn maze **after dark**. The Night Maze runs with very little lighting, so bring a flashlight or glow stick and stick with the group as you try to find your way out — a genuinely different experience from wandering a maze at 2 p.m.

It's a fun, low-stakes fall outing that's more novel than the usual daytime pumpkin patch trip, and works fine solo, as a couple, or with the whole crew.

Bob's Corn is out past Woodinville in Snohomish — about a **40-minute drive from downtown Redmond**, heading north away from the city rather than into it, so no Seattle-traffic tax here. Note that your Gearhouse RSVP doesn't include farm admission; you'll pay Bob's Corn directly for maze entry.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) to join the group.
