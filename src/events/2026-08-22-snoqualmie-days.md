**Snoqualmie Days** is exactly the kind of small-town-festival charm that's fun to drop in on even without a personal tie to the town: a free, all-day celebration along car-free **Railroad Avenue** on **Saturday, August 22**. The day kicks off with a **pancake breakfast** at the fire station (7-11 a.m.), followed by a **grand parade** at 11, vendor booths, a model railroad show at City Hall, a LEGO show, a kids' field of fun, and train rides at the historic Snoqualmie Depot at 11, 1, and 3. Things wind down with a **music crawl** through downtown shops and bars starting at 3:30 p.m.

It's a **25-30 minute drive** east from downtown Redmond via I-90 — a step past the usual Eastside loop, but an easy one, and a genuinely fun small-town outing for families or anyone who likes a parade and a train ride. If you want to make a weekend of it, the festival kicks off Friday evening with a wine train excursion at the Northwest Railway Museum (see our separate write-up) — pair the two for a full Eastside day trip.

Full schedule at [snoqualmiedays.org](https://snoqualmiedays.org/schedule-of-events/).
