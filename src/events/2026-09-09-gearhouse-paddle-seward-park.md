Gearhouse's **Paddle Club** heads to **Seward Park** on **Wednesday, September 9** for a post-work session on Lake Washington — boards and kayaks provided, evening light, and a relaxed pace built more for hanging out on the water than racking up miles. Seward Park's peninsula shoreline is sheltered enough for a calm paddle even if the main lake has some chop.

Like the club's other Lake Washington and Lake Union outings, this is a weeknight trip into Seattle — roughly a 25-minute drive from downtown Redmond. Boat transport is included for Summit members; other membership tiers should check the RSVP details for what's covered.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) for the exact meet spot and gear logistics. Bring a swimsuit, a towel, and a dry bag for your phone.
