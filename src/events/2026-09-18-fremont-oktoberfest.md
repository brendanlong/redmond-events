Seattle's **Fremont Oktoberfest** returns for its 21-and-over weekend, **September 18–20**: Friday evening 5–11pm, Saturday noon–11pm, Sunday noon–5pm. Expect the classic format — a big beer garden with dozens of taps, German food, and live music, sprawled across the Fremont neighborhood.

A straightforward, high-yield pick for the "food events" crowd — beer festivals are exactly the kind of thing worth a trip into the city, and the three-day window gives plenty of flexibility to pick a time that works (Sunday afternoon is the mellower option if a rowdier Saturday night isn't your speed).

It's about a 30-minute drive to Fremont. General admission is $10, with tasting/stein passes running $25–$49 depending on how much beer you want included. Details at [fremontoktoberfest.com](https://fremontoktoberfest.com/).
