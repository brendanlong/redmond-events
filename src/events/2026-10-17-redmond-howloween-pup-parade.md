Downtown Redmond's second annual **Howl-O-Ween** takes over Redmond Town Center on **Saturday, October 17, from noon to 4 p.m.** Dress up your dog for a parade through the center, then compete in the costume contest, with prizes for best dog costume, best pet-and-human duo, and best in show.

After the parade, the party moves to the Hilton Garden Inn Seattle Redmond for snacks and drinks, and you can meet adoptable pups from Motley Zoo Animal Rescue; a portion of proceeds supports the rescue's work. It's in the middle of downtown, so it's an easy walk or a short drive for readers and a good excuse to wander the Town Center afterward. Details are on the [Redmond Town Center events page](https://redmondtowncenter.com/events/).
