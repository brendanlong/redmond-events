On **Thursday, October 29**, the Redmond Senior & Community Center hosts a line dance evening led by instructor Evamarie: part lesson, part dance party, set to music from a mix of genres. All skill levels are welcome and you don't need a partner, which makes it one of the lowest-pressure ways to try dancing in town.

It's in the heart of Redmond on 160th Avenue NE, a short walk from downtown. Sign up through [Redmond Parks and Recreation](https://www.redmond.gov/ParksRecreation). The City's newsletter didn't give a start time or price, so check the registration page; the 6:30 p.m. time here is an estimate for the calendar entry.
