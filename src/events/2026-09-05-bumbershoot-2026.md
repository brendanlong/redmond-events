Seattle's **Bumbershoot Arts & Music Festival** is back at Seattle Center for its 53rd year, over Labor Day weekend, **Saturday and Sunday, September 5–6**. Two days under the Space Needle covering music, visual art, fashion, comedy, wrestling, and food, with the music lineup led by **Death Cab for Cutie** doing a hometown set, plus Turnstile, Blood Orange, Lucy Dacus, Bikini Kill, De La Soul, Peaches, TOKiMONSTA, Noname, and a long list of others spanning genres and generations.

Death Cab for Cutie alone makes this worth flagging — they're on the "wanted" list for readers already — but the format is the real draw: Bumbershoot has always been more of a discovery festival than a headliner showcase, and the multi-stage, multi-discipline setup (art, comedy, wrestling, food alongside the music) makes it easy to wander between things rather than committing to one lineup all day.

It's a genuine trip into the city — Seattle Center is about a **30-minute drive from downtown Redmond**, and a Labor Day weekend festival means real crowds and parking pressure (light rail into Westlake plus a walk, or the monorail, is worth considering over driving). But this is exactly the kind of big, one-weekend-a-year Seattle institution that clears the higher city bar.

Tickets and the full schedule at [bumbershoot.com](https://www.bumbershoot.com/).
