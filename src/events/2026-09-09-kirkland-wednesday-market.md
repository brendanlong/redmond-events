The **Kirkland Wednesday Market** is in its final weeks of the 2026 season, running 3–7pm on the waterfront at Marina Park every Wednesday through **September 30** (upcoming dates: the 9th, 16th, 23rd, and 30th). It's a straightforward farmers-market lineup — produce, flowers, and specialty food vendors — right on the Kirkland lakefront.

A direct hit on the farmers-market interest our readers keep gravitating toward, and a nice excuse for an evening on the water before the season wraps up. It's about a **15-minute drive to downtown Kirkland**, easy enough for a weeknight stop on the way home.

Details at [kirklanddowntown.org](https://kirklanddowntown.org/events/kirkland-market/).
