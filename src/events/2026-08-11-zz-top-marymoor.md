**ZZ Top** plays Marymoor Park on **Tuesday, August 11**, with **Cheap Trick** opening — doors at 4 p.m., show starts at 6 p.m. Two bona fide arena-rock veterans on one bill: ZZ Top's boogie-blues riffs ("Sharp Dressed Man," "Legs") paired with Cheap Trick's power-pop hooks ("Surrender," "I Want You to Want Me"). It's a crowd-pleasing, sing-along kind of night rather than a deep cut show.

It's a Tuesday, but Marymoor being walk distance takes the "is a weeknight show worth it" question off the table entirely — no parking hassle, no long drive home, just a short walk back into Redmond after.
