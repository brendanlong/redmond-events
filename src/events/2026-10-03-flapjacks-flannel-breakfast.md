The City of Redmond's 2nd Annual **Flapjacks & Flannel Breakfast** hits the Redmond Senior & Community Center on **Saturday, October 3, from 9:30 a.m. to noon** — a pancake breakfast that rolls into seasonal snacks, games, crafts, and activities for all ages. Wear your best flannel for a shot at a fall-themed raffle prize.

It's an easy walk or short drive from downtown Redmond, and about as low-key and family-friendly as fall events get: free-ish (check the registration link for cost), casual, and geared for kids and adults alike. Register ahead so the city can plan for enough pancakes.

Details and registration via [redmond.gov/ParksRecreation](https://www.redmond.gov/ParksRecreation).
