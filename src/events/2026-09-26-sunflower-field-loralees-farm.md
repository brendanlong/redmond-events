**Lora Lee's Family Farm** in Duvall is running its 100-acre sunflower field and maze — staggered plantings meant to keep blooms going through the end of September, so this is close to the last call for the season. Besides the maze itself, there's a u-cut cutting garden (dahlias, zinnias, sunflowers by the stem), a weekend petting zoo with mini donkeys, chickens, and goats, and plenty of photo-op spots among the flowers.

It's about a **20-25 minute drive from downtown Redmond** out toward Duvall — an easy eastside outing, good for families or just a fall-afternoon wander through actual sunflowers instead of a pumpkin patch for once. Open **Thursday through Monday, 10 a.m. – 7 p.m.** (closed Tuesday/Wednesday); admission is charged at the barn, ages 4 and under free.

Check [loraleesfamilyfarm.com](https://www.loraleesfamilyfarm.com/sunflowermaze) for bloom updates before heading out, since timing depends on weather — and go soon, as the season wraps up at the end of the month.
