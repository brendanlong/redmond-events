Gearhouse is heading to **Boulderfest** at Seattle Bouldering Project's Poplar location on **Saturday, September 26, at 6 p.m.** to watch the Open Finals — the top climbers in the region cutting loose on competition problems. It's not just a quiet spectator thing, either: expect a vendor market, games, beer from West Wall, flash tattoos, and a genuinely festival-sized crowd from the Seattle climbing community.

For anyone who's watched the Gearhouse crew head to Exit 38 on Thursdays and wondered what competitive climbing actually looks like, this is the fun, low-commitment way to find out — no gear or membership at SBP required to spectate. SBP Poplar is in Wallingford, about a 25–30 minute drive from Redmond via SR 520.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) if you want to roll with the group.
