Twelve-piece blues-rock powerhouse **Tedeschi Trucks Band** — led by guitarist Derek Trucks and singer/guitarist Susan Tedeschi — bring their "Future Soul" tour to Chateau Ste. Michelle's outdoor amphitheater for **two nights back to back, August 4th and 5th**, with singer-songwriter Lukas Nelson (son of Willie) opening both shows.

If your taste in "live music, broad range" runs toward musicianship over spectacle, this is a band built for it: horns, keys, backup singers, twin drummers, and two lead guitarists trading solos, playing a mix of soul, blues, and Southern rock that rewards actually paying attention. It's the kind of act that turns a winery lawn into a genuine listening room for a couple hours.

Same deal both nights: showtime is **6 p.m.**, with doors about 90 minutes earlier for anyone who wants a full picnic setup on the lawn. The venue is in Woodinville, roughly **15 minutes from downtown Redmond** via Redmond–Woodinville Road, with offsite shuttle parking available — an easy one to just decide same-day for either date. Tickets through Ticketmaster; check the [full summer lineup](https://www.ste-michelle.com/concerts/summer-concerts) for what else is coming up.
