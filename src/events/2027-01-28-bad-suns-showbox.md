L.A. indie rockers **Bad Suns** play The Showbox on **Thursday, January 28, 2027, at 8 p.m.**, celebrating the 10th anniversary of their album *Disappear Here* — surfy, upbeat guitar pop in the vein of the indie/pop-leaning end of the local taste around here. Expect the album played through plus a set of fan favorites.

The Showbox is downtown on 1st Avenue, about a 30-minute drive from Redmond via SR 520 or I-90. It's a Thursday-night show, so plan around evening commute traffic heading into the city and budget for downtown parking or take the bus in.

Tickets and details at [showboxpresents.com](https://www.showboxpresents.com/).
