A brand-new four-day psych/garage/punk festival is landing in Pioneer Square this fall: **Baba Obscura**, running **November 5–8** across six stages at Baba Yaga, The Showbox, Axis, and Central Saloon. The lineup so far includes The Black Angels, Provoker, Mdou Moctar, Black Mountain, and Night Beats, with more acts expected to be announced.

A debut festival with an already-legit touring lineup is worth flagging early — this is squarely the kind of "novel and fun" live-music event that's worth building a Seattle trip around, even outside the usual named-artist wishlist. Multi-venue, walkable-between-stages festivals in Pioneer Square tend to have a good crowd and discovery-friendly format.

It's two months out, so no rush — but if a multi-day pass is something you'd want, it's worth getting on the radar now before the lineup (and prices) firm up. Details at [babaobscura.com](https://babaobscura.com/).
