Downtown Issaquah's free **Confluence Music Festival** returns to Confluence Park on **Sunday, August 30, from 2 to 7 p.m.** — its 8th year running. It's a laid-back afternoon of live jazz, blues, and rock from a mix of local and touring acts, plus food trucks, a beer and wine garden, and an art component (plein-air painters working live, an interactive "Strings of Change" installation) that gives it more texture than a standard park concert.

Issaquah is a straightforward Eastside drive — about 20 minutes from downtown Redmond via I-90 — and this is exactly the kind of free, low-commitment community festival that's worth the trip even without a specific artist pulling you in: good weather, an easy afternoon out, and no ticket to buy.

More details at [downtownissaquah.com](https://downtownissaquah.com/events/confluence-music-festival-2026/).
