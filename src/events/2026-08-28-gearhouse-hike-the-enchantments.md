For the serious hikers: Gearhouse is running a full weekend trip to do **the Enchantments** as a single-day thru-hike — 18 miles, roughly 6,500 feet of elevation gain, and about 13 hours on trail, crossing one of the most sought-after (and hardest-to-get-into) alpine landscapes in the Cascades: granite basins, turquoise tarns, larches, and mountain goats, without needing to win the backcountry-camping permit lottery since the group bases out of a Leavenworth Airbnb instead of camping in the core zone.

This is a genuine step up from Gearhouse's usual day hikes — closer in scale to a mountaineering objective than a stroll. The group drives out **Friday afternoon, August 28th**, and stays over in Leavenworth ($100-150 per person, split lodging). **Saturday morning**, everyone meets at the **Snow Lake trailhead** (Alpental) at **5:30 a.m.**, then shuttles over to the Stuart Lake side to start the traverse at 6:15 — hiking essentially all day before arriving back at Snow Lake around 7 p.m. Sunday is a slow recovery morning and the drive home. There's a planning meeting back at the Gearhouse coffee shop on **August 10th** for anyone considering it, covering gear and logistics in detail.

The honest version: this is a big commitment — a full weekend, real money for lodging, and a hike that will wreck your legs for a few days after — but the Enchantments are genuinely exceptional, the kind of place that's worth planning a whole trip around rather than a place you casually swing by. It's about a 2-hour drive from Redmond to Snoqualmie Pass to start.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/). Bring full day-hike gear (10 essentials, layers, real food) even though you're not camping — 13 hours on exposed alpine trail is not a place to be underprepared.
