Gearhouse is taking the club off the mainland for a change: a **Friday-to-Sunday campout at Odlin County Park on Lopez Island**, Sept. 11-13. Lopez is the mellowest of the San Juans — rolling farmland, quiet beaches, no stoplights on the whole island — and Odlin is a waterfront county park a short walk from the ferry landing, so it's an easy first island campout if you haven't done one.

This is a genuine trip: a drive to Anacortes, a ferry crossing, then a weekend of camping, biking, and beach time with the Gearhouse crew. That's real effort for a Redmond reader, but a chill car-free-feeling island weekend is the kind of thing that's hard to replicate with a day trip, and it's the sort of "actually get away" outing worth the logistics once a summer.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) for carpool coordination, ferry timing, and campsite details.
