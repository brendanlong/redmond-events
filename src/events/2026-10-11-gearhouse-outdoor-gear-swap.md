Gearhouse and Standard Brewing are hosting a Seattle **Outdoor Gear Swap** on **Sunday, October 11, from 12:30 to 5:30 p.m.** at Standard Brewing, at 25th Ave and Jackson St in the Central District. Locals sell, swap, and hunt for quality used outdoor gear, with a brewery on site.

It's a weekend afternoon, so Seattle parking and traffic are less of a hurdle than on a weeknight, though it is a roughly 30-minute drive from downtown Redmond. A good stop if you need hiking or paddling gear before winter; since readers are Gearhouse members, check the Gearhouse calendar for details on selling a table or just browsing.
