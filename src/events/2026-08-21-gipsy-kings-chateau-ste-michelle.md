The band behind "Bamboleo" and "Djobi Djoba" — **Gipsy Kings**, led by **Nicolas Reyes** — bring their flamenco-rumba sound to the Chateau Ste. Michelle lawn on **Friday, August 21**. This is the kind of show that's more festival than concert: propulsive Spanish guitar, a full band, and a crowd that tends to be on its feet and dancing rather than sitting on blankets — a nice change of pace from the singer-songwriter fare that dominates most of this venue's summer.

Gates open around **5:30 p.m.** for the **7 p.m.** start. It's a **15-20 minute drive** out to Woodinville from downtown Redmond, the usual easy Chateau trip, but worth planning to be there early if you want room to move rather than just sit.

Tickets and full details at [ste-michelle.com](https://www.ste-michelle.com/concerts/summer-concerts), via Ticketmaster.
