Swiss folk metal outfit **Eluveitie** — hurdy-gurdy, fiddle, and flutes stitched into blast-beat metal — plays El Corazon on **Friday, February 27, 2027, at 7 p.m.** Tickets just went on sale. If Alestorm or Amaranthe are in your rotation, Eluveitie sits right alongside them: Celtic-inspired folk melodies riding on top of genuinely heavy metal, not just a novelty crossover.

El Corazon is on Eastlake, a roughly 25–30 minute drive from downtown Redmond (SR 520 into the city), with a general-admission club floor and a bar. It's a Friday night, so traffic in should be lighter than a weeknight commute, but budget extra time for parking near South Lake Union.

Tickets and details at [elcorazonseattle.com](https://www.elcorazonseattle.com/).
