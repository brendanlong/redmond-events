Redmond Parks & Recreation is turning the horse-farm park on the edge of town into a dog-friendly Halloween party. **Bark in the Park: Howl-o-ween** runs **Friday, October 9, from 4 to 5:30 p.m.** at Farrel-McWhirter Park, with an after-hours barnyard stroll, a trick-or-treat hunt, a selfie station, and a costume parade.

It's a short drive east of downtown Redmond (about 10 minutes), and the park's pastures and barns make it a more charming backdrop than the usual parking-lot costume contest. Bring a dog in costume, or just come for the farm animals. Registration is handled through the city's recreation portal, linked from [redmond.gov/ParksRecreation](https://www.redmond.gov/ParksRecreation).
