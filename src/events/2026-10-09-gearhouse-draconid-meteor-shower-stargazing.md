Gearhouse is teaming up with the **Seattle Astronomical Society** for a public **Draconid meteor shower stargazing night** at Big Rock Sports Field in Duvall on **Friday, October 9**. Experienced SAS astronomers bring telescopes and binoculars and help point out what's overhead; bring your own gear too if you have it. It's free, and open to non-members as well as the club.

A nice, low-key novelty for a fall Friday — dark-sky stargazing with people who actually know the sky, rather than just craning your neck in a parking lot. The Draconids peak in early-to-mid October and are known for occasional strong outbursts, so it's a reasonable gamble for a real show.

Big Rock Sports Field is out past Redmond in Duvall — about a **20-minute drive** from downtown Redmond, and genuinely dark out there compared to anywhere closer in. Dress warm; late-night October outside runs cold. The event goes until 12:30 a.m., but there's no obligation to stay the whole time.

Details on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/).
