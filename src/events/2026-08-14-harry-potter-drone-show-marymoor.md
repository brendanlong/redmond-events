1,200 illuminated drones recreate scenes and characters from the **Harry Potter** films over Marymoor Park on the nights of **Friday, Aug. 14 and Saturday, Aug. 15**, set to the films' score with a live violinist. Doors (well, gates) open about two hours early for a wizarding-world-themed pre-show — food, drinks, and merch — before the roughly hour-long drone display starts at **9:15 p.m.**

It's a novelty rather than a traditional concert, but this kind of large-scale drone show doesn't come through often, and doing it at Marymoor means the usual walk-distance ease applies — no parking scrum for what's otherwise a genuinely big production. Good for Potter fans of any age, and probably a memorable one for kids who are into the movies, though the 9:15 p.m. start makes it a late night for younger ones.

Tickets run **$36-$96** depending on viewing area; get them through [Fever](https://feverup.com/m/621540) or see [thedroneartshow.com](https://thedroneartshow.com/seattle/harry-potter/) for the full rundown.
