A folk-and-power-metal double bill is headed to El Corazon on **Wednesday, March 24, 2027**: **Týr**, the Faroese band that sets old Nordic ballads and sea-shanty-style chants to big, galloping heavy metal, co-headlining with Swedish power-metal outfit **Dynazty**. Tickets just went on sale.

For readers who've been to Alestorm and Amaranthe shows, this is squarely in the pocket. Týr is the folkier, chant-along half — songs sung partly in Faroese, with a Viking-saga theme that makes for a fun crowd — while Dynazty brings the soaring, melodic power-metal choruses (their singer also fronts Amaranthe). Two bands that each sound good on their own, and that complement each other better than most tour pairings.

El Corazon is on Eastlake at the south end of Lake Union, a roughly 25–30 minute drive from downtown Redmond via SR 520 — though this is a **Wednesday**, so plan for commute traffic if you head in before 7 and expect a late drive home on a work night. The venue is a general-admission club with a bar; shows there usually start around 7 p.m. (check the listing for exact door times and age limits).

Details and tickets at [elcorazonseattle.com](https://www.elcorazonseattle.com/).
