The fall follow-up to Gearhouse's summer berry-picking trip: a relaxed morning of **apple and pumpkin picking at Swans Trail Farms** on **Sunday, October 11**. The plan is the same easy formula — wander the orchard and pumpkin patch, pick your own, and come home with a haul for pie, cider, or the front porch.

It's a genuinely family-friendly, low-effort fall outing — no elevation gain required, just a farm, fresh air, and a good excuse to get out of the house on an October Sunday.

Swans Trail Farms sits just outside Snohomish, about a **40-minute drive from downtown Redmond** up past Woodinville — out into farm country rather than into the city. You pay the farm directly for what you pick, on top of membership, so bring cash or a card.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/). Wagon rides and a corn maze are typically part of the farm's fall setup if you want to make a longer visit of it.
