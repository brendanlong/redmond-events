**Ben Folds Five** — the piano-bass-drums trio behind "Brick," "Song for the Dumped," and "Army" — is back together for its first tour in well over a decade, and the Northwest stop is an outdoor summer show at **Remlinger Farms** in Carnation on **Friday, July 23, 2027**.

Ben Folds is a natural fit for readers who love nerd-rock songwriters like Jonathan Coulton: funny, a little sardonic, piano-forward, and sneakily heartfelt. Seeing the original trio (rather than a Ben Folds solo set) is the draw here — the band hasn't toured since 2013, so this may be a rare chance. Remlinger's concert field is also a much nicer way to see a show than an arena: grass, valley views, and a relaxed summer-evening vibe.

Carnation is a scenic 20–25 minute drive east of downtown Redmond along Redmond-Fall City Road and SR 203 — no city traffic or downtown parking, just the slow two-lane road out to the valley and lots of cars leaving at once afterward. Bring layers for after sunset. Exact door and set times weren't posted yet; outdoor shows there typically open in the early evening.

Tickets went on sale November 20; see [remlingerfarms.com](https://remlingerfarms.com/) for details.
