Marymoor Park is getting a genuinely rare landmark this month: organizers behind the **Washington Ganesh Festival** (Beats of Washington) say they're installing the tallest Ganesha Murti in the US — a 15-foot idol — alongside a full Ram Mandir-style installation, for a three-day celebration running **Friday, September 18 through Sunday, September 20**.

Expect the full spread of a major Ganesh Chaturthi celebration: live music and dance performances, a shopping bazaar, food, and a grand procession to close things out on Sunday. If you enjoyed the Holi celebration at Marymoor, this is cut from the same cloth — a big, colorful cultural festival that's worth a look even if you have no personal connection to the tradition.

It's about as easy a yes as this newsletter gets: Marymoor is walking distance for most of our readers, and free general admission (with registration) means no cost barrier either. Exact daily programming hasn't been published in full — Friday evening looks like an opening/kickoff session, with Saturday and Sunday as the bigger full-day events — so check the [official event page](https://beatsofwa.org/events/ganesh-festival-redmond-2026) closer to the date for the detailed schedule and to register.
