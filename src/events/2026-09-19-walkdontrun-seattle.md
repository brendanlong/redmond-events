**WALKDONTRUN** turns 20 blocks of downtown Seattle sidewalks, plazas, and parks into a free, one-day public art crawl on **Saturday, September 19, from 11 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.** More than 200 local artists spread out from **Westlake Park** to Occidental Square, turning storefronts, sidewalks, and plazas into stages for visual art, live music, and performance — the kind of surprise-around-every-corner event that rewards just wandering rather than following a set schedule.

You don't need to do the whole route; drop in for a few blocks near wherever you park, or commit to the whole 20-block stretch. Free tickets are available in advance, and organizers ask attendees to bring a canned good for the Pike Market Food Bank when starting at Westlake Park. It's a ~30-minute drive from Redmond with the usual downtown parking hassle, but a large-scale, free, one-day-only public art event is exactly the kind of novel outing worth making the trip for.

Free tickets and full route details at [walkdontrun-sea.org](https://walkdontrun-sea.org/).
