Gearhouse is spending the day at **Stocker Farms'** Fall Festival on **Sunday, October 25** — a full-service fall outing with a U-pick pumpkin patch, a corn maze, and more than 30 other farm attractions (jumping pillows, zip lines, pig races, photo spots at the pumpkin barn). Since it's a Sunday, weekend-only extras are in play too: hayrides, the pumpkin cannon, and a beer, wine, and cider garden, plus food trucks on-site.

It's a step up from a quick pumpkin-picking trip — plan to spend the better part of the day there — and it's the kind of easy, family-friendly fall outing that needs nothing more than showing up ready to wander a farm for a few hours.

Stocker Farms sits in Snohomish, right by Bob's Corn — about a **40-minute drive from downtown Redmond**, north past Woodinville rather than into Seattle. Gearhouse RSVP gets you in the group; admission to the farm itself is separate, paid directly to Stocker Farms.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) to join the group, or just meet everyone there.
