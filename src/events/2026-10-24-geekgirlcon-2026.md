**GeekGirlCon** returns to the Seattle Convention Center for its 2026 run, **Saturday and
Sunday, October 24–25** — a two-day convention built around women and all-comers in geek
culture, with the kind of eclectic programming that makes it worth a special trip rather
than a routine con: **Dungeons and Drag Queens** (three drag queens playing D&D live, with
a dungeon-master comedian and an improv violinist), the con's first-ever **Idol Fest** (a
live-performance idol-culture show hosted by local performer Magical Melody), portfolio
reviews from actual Dungeons & Dragons and Magic: The Gathering art teams, a hockey-fandom
panel, a vendor hall, and a full gaming hall with both video and tabletop games running all
weekend.

It's a genuinely nerdy, playful event — the kind of thing that should appeal if trivia
nights, tabletop games, or nerd-rock artists like Jonathan Coulton or I Fight Dragons are
your speed — and it's run entirely by volunteers as a non-profit, so ticket money goes
straight back into the con and the community around it.

It's a trip into downtown Seattle, about a **30-minute drive** from Redmond (longer if you
catch weekend event traffic downtown), which is the honest cost of admission for a
Seattle-bar event — but a two-day geek convention with drag-queen D&D and an idol-culture
concert clears that bar. Both days run all-ages during the day; check the schedule for
exact panel times once it's posted. Passes are on sale now at
[geekgirlcon.com](https://geekgirlcon.com/).
