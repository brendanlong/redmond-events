For the runners: a member-led **Wednesday Night Run Club** meets at the Gearhouse coffee shop and heads out together for an easy group run around Capitol Hill. It's casual and low-commitment — no pace requirements, just a group heading out the door together and back for a post-run hang.

Straightforward rec-sports-style social exercise, the same spirit as a casual dodgeball league or pickup game — just running instead. Upcoming Wednesdays on the calendar include **September 2, September 16, and October 7**, all at 6 p.m.

Same honest note as the other Capitol Hill weeknight Gearhouse events: this is a trip into Seattle on a weeknight, about a **25–30 minute drive from downtown Redmond**. Worth it if a group run and some post-run socializing sounds like a good way to spend a Wednesday evening; the schedule keeps rolling most Wednesdays.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/), or contact the group in Slack for specifics.
