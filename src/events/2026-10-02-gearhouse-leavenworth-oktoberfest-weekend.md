Gearhouse is running a full **choose-your-own-adventure weekend in Leavenworth**, **Friday, October 2 through Sunday, October 4**, timed to hit peak larch season alongside Leavenworth's **Oktoberfest**. The group shares a cabin or vacation home in the Leavenworth area for two nights, with Saturday left open for members to pick their own pace: go big with an alpine hike toward the Enchantments or Colchuck Lake for the golden larches, take an easier trip out to look for fall color, or just spend the day at Oktoberfest in town.

This is a step up from Gearhouse's usual day trips — a real weekend commitment with shared lodging costs (roughly $100-150 per person) on top of membership — but it lands during the two-week window each fall when the larches turn gold, which is exactly the kind of trip worth planning around rather than dropping in on casually. It's about a 2.5-hour drive from Redmond to Leavenworth if you'd rather meet the group there than join the Friday shuttle from the Capitol Hill shop.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) for cabin assignments, carpool coordination, and which Saturday adventure you're signing up for. Check [leavenworth.org/oktoberfest](https://leavenworth.org/oktoberfest/) for the festival's own schedule if that's the draw.
