A gentle way to kick off Halloween: Gearhouse is leading a casual group hike to **Cherry
Creek Falls** near Duvall on **Saturday, October 31**, meeting at the trailhead at
**8 a.m.** It's an easy one — about 5 miles out-and-back with only 450 feet of elevation
gain — through moody, mossy Pacific Northwest forest to a 25-foot waterfall, at a relaxed
pace built for chatting rather than pushing hard.

This one's actually closer to Redmond than it is to Gearhouse's own Capitol Hill home base —
about a **25-minute drive** out SR-202 to Duvall, versus a much longer haul in from Seattle.
Parking is limited to the shoulder of Mountain View Road, so carpooling or arriving early is
worth it.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/).
