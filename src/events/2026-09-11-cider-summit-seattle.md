**Cider Summit Seattle** marks its 15th year at the South Lake Union lawn on **September 11 (3–8pm, VIP entry at 2pm) and September 12 (noon–5pm)** — dozens of craft cideries pouring tasting-sized pours in one outdoor setting.

Another solid match for the food-and-drink-festival interest, and cider is different enough from the usual beer-fest circuit to be worth a look even if you've done a few of those already. South Lake Union is walkable from Westlake if you're taking transit in, which softens the usual Seattle parking headache.

About a 30-minute drive into the city. Tickets ($35–$55 GA, $45–$50+ VIP) at [cidersummitnw.com](https://www.cidersummitnw.com/seattle1).
