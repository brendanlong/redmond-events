A gentler outing for the fall-color crowd: Gearhouse is meeting up at **Kubota Garden** on
**Sunday, October 18**, for a relaxed afternoon walk through the 20-acre Japanese garden in
south Seattle — winding paths, ponds, bridges, and a colorful collection of Japanese maples
that peak right around this time of year. This is a stroll, not a hike, so it's an easy sell
for anyone who wants the fall-foliage payoff without the elevation gain.

Kubota Garden is free and open to the public, so no membership or RSVP is strictly required
to just show up and enjoy it — Gearhouse is simply using it as a meetup point. It's about a
**25–30 minute drive** from Redmond via I-405 and I-90 south into Rainier Beach.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) if you want to walk with
the group; otherwise the garden's open daily, dawn to dusk.
