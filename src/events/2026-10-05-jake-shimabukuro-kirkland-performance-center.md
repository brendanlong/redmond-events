Ukulele virtuoso **Jake Shimabukuro** plays Kirkland Performance Center on **Monday, October 5, at 7 p.m.** Often billed as the "Jimi Hendrix of the ukulele," he stretches a four-string instrument across jazz, rock, classical, blues, folk, and Hawaiian traditions. If you've only heard his famous take on "While My Guitar Gently Weeps," a live set is a very different experience.

Kirkland is about a 20-minute drive from downtown Redmond, and the theater is right in the walkable downtown core. It's short notice (tomorrow night), so check ticket availability. Tickets are listed via [Explore Kirkland](https://www.explorekirkland.com/events/) and the Kirkland Performance Center.
