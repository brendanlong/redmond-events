English pop legend **Lily Allen** — of "Smile" and "Not Fair" fame — comes through the Paramount Theatre on **Thursday, September 24th**, playing her new album *West End Girl* front to back. Tickets just went on sale. The record is a sharp-tongued, autobiographical divorce album (there's already a companion documentary announced), which tracks with Allen's whole career: witty, blunt British pop that doesn't flinch from the messy details.

For anyone whose taste runs to pop with some bite to it rather than pure sugar, this is a rare full-album performance from an artist who doesn't tour the US often — worth grabbing tickets early rather than waiting.

It's a trip into the city on a weeknight (budget for parking, or take light rail to Westlake and walk a couple blocks), but a headline theater show from an artist this well-known clears that bar. Tickets and details at [stgpresents.org](https://www.stgpresents.org/paramount).
