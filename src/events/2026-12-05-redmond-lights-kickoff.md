Redmond's winter light festival is back, and the kick-off party is **Saturday, December 5, from 4 to 8 p.m. at Downtown Park**. Expect large illuminated art installations, live music and dance on multiple stages, free craft booths, artificial "snowfall," a Kids Light Fashion Show, and the Family Lights Promenade, a stroll along a glowing walkway.

The Luminary Trail, a path of lanterns and lights, leads from the park over to Redmond Town Center, so you can pair it with dinner or shopping. This year's Art + Light pieces include a woven, LED-lit "mountain" landscape, an infinity-mirror piece you can navigate, a five-panel pop-art portrait of Redmond, and a data-driven light piece built from Redmond's streams, trails and parks.

If the kick-off crowd isn't your thing, the installations at Downtown Park and Esterra Park stay up through **January 4, 2027**, so you can see them on a quiet evening. It's the definition of the walking-distance tier: for readers about 20 minutes' walk from downtown, you can stroll there and skip parking. Dress warmly; December evenings are cold and often wet. Schedule, artist list and a map are on the [city's Redmond Lights page](https://www.redmond.gov/1139/Redmond-Lights).
