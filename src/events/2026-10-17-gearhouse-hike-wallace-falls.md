A more moderate option for the hiking crowd, running the same Saturday as Gearhouse's bigger
Lake Ingalls trip: a group hike to **Wallace Falls**, one of Washington's most photogenic
waterfalls, on **Saturday, October 17**. The Woody Trail follows the Wallace River through
lush forest past a series of viewpoints before climbing to the Lower, Middle, and Upper
Falls — a solid but manageable 5.6 miles round trip with about 1,300 feet of elevation gain,
hiked together at a steady, social pace with breaks along the way.

Gearhouse's Capitol Hill crew carpools out at **7:30 a.m.**; it's roughly a **55-minute
drive** out US 2 to Gold Bar, putting the group at the trailhead around **8:25 a.m.** From
Redmond, it's a similar drive via SR-522 and US 2 — easier to head straight out than to
detour into Seattle first.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) — a Discover Pass is
required to park at the state park trailhead.
