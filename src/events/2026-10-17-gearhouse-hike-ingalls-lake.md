A serious one for the hiking crowd: Gearhouse is leading a group hike to **Lake Ingalls** on **Saturday, October 17**, timed for peak larch season in the Teanaway. It's rated challenging — about 9 miles round trip with roughly 2,800 feet of elevation gain and an estimated 6 hours on trail — climbing up into the Esmeralda Basin to a turquoise alpine lake ringed by golden larches and views of Mount Stuart. Bring lunch; it's a long day.

This one's genuinely out there — well past the Eastside and into the Teanaway drainage near Cle Elum — so it clears the "farther afield" bar only because larch season is a short, can't-miss window each fall.

The **time listed above is when the group meets at the trailhead**: the Capitol Hill carpool leaves at **7:30 a.m.**, and it's about a 2.5-hour drive out from Seattle, putting the group at the trailhead around **10 a.m.** Redmond hikers are much better off skipping the trip into the city and driving straight out via I-90 and Highway 970 to meet at the trailhead directly. Non-Summit members need to arrange their own transportation.

RSVP on the [Gearhouse calendar](https://www.joingearhouse.com/) — a Northwest Forest Pass (or America the Beautiful Pass) is required to park.
