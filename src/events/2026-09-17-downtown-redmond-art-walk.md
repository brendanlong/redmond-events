The fourth annual **Downtown Redmond Art Walk (DRAW)** turns downtown into a one-evening open-air gallery on **Thursday, Sept. 17, 4-7 p.m.** Downtown Park gets temporary sculptures, live mural painting, and poetry readings curated by the City's Cultural Arts Program, while participating businesses along the walk host their own exhibitions, live music, and poetry readings for local artists. A map for a self-guided tour of the participating storefronts will be available on the night.

It's the kind of low-commitment evening event that's easy to wander into after work or dinner — no tickets, no set schedule, just walk the loop and see what's out. Downtown Park is dead center of downtown Redmond, so this is as walkable as it gets. Details and the eventual business map will be posted at [redmond.gov/DRAW](https://www.redmond.gov/DRAW).
