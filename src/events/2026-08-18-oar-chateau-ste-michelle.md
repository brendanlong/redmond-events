**O.A.R.** rolls through the Chateau Ste. Michelle lawn on **Tuesday, August 18** as part of their **Three Decades Tour**, marking 30 years of a band that's built a loyal following on jam-band-adjacent rock with reggae and pop hooks — think "Love and Memories" and "Shattered (Turn the Car Around)." **Gavin DeGraw** and the **Pernikoff Brothers** open. It's a good pick for anyone who came up on 2000s college-rock radio or just wants a singalong-friendly summer evening.

Gates open around **5 p.m.** for the **6:30 p.m.** start — the usual winery-lawn format, so bring a blanket or low chairs and get there early for good lawn real estate. Woodinville is a **15-20 minute drive** from downtown Redmond, an easy weeknight trip for a band with this much of a following.

Tickets and full details at [ste-michelle.com](https://www.ste-michelle.com/concerts/summer-concerts), via Ticketmaster.
