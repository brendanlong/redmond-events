If you've been meaning to turn casual pickleball into something with a bit of stakes, the City of Redmond is starting an **Adult Pickleball League (Doubles)** on **Monday, October 26**, at the **Old Redmond School House**. Matches run weekly, building to a playoff on **December 14**.

It's open to adults of all skill levels and welcomes mixed and non-mixed doubles teams. Rosters are flexible: you can add up to two substitutes, which helps with the inevitable scheduling conflicts of a ten-week season. Sign up with a partner (or a full crew) through the city's registration page.

The Old Redmond School House sits at the edge of downtown, an easy walk or a very short drive for most readers, so it's about as low-friction as league sports get. The city's newsletter didn't list exact weekly match times or the fee, and the start time above is our placeholder for an evening slot, so check the registration page before you commit. For drop-in play without a season commitment, the weekly Thursday pickleball at the same building is still running.
