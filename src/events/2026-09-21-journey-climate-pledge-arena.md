Classic-rock arena headliner **Journey** plays Climate Pledge Arena on **Monday, September 21** (doors 6pm, show 7:30pm) on their Final Frontier Tour.

Not every act clears Seattle's "particularly interesting" bar, but a full arena production from a band this well-known is a reasonable exception — "Don't Stop Believin'" is the kind of song that turns a crowd into a singalong regardless of how ironic you go in.

It's a Monday night into the city (about a 30-minute drive, with arena parking to plan around), so this one's for readers who already know they want the big-stage experience. Tickets from ~$97 via [climatepledgearena.com](https://climatepledgearena.com/event/journey-26/).
