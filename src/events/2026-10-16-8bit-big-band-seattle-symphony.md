The multi-GRAMMY-winning **8-Bit Big Band** teams up with the **Seattle Symphony** on **Friday, October 16, at 7:30 p.m.** for a night of video game music reimagined as full orchestral jazz — the kind of genre-defying arrangement treatment that turns 8-bit chiptunes into something a full concert hall can play. If Jonathan Coulton or I Fight Dragons are your speed, this is squarely the same nerdy-music-nerd wheelhouse, just with a symphony orchestra behind it instead of a rock band.

Benaroya Hall is downtown, about a 30-minute drive from Redmond via SR 520 (or bus/light rail if you'd rather skip parking). It's a Friday night show, so traffic in should be lighter than a weeknight commute.

Tickets and details at [seattlesymphony.org](https://www.seattlesymphony.org/).
