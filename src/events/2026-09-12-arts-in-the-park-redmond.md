**Arts in the Park** wraps up its September run with free Saturday-evening performances at Redmond's Downtown Park, 4–8pm on **September 12, 19, and 26**. The lineup rotates week to week — past weeks have brought K-pop dance crews, theater troupes, and multicultural dance showcases — alongside an "Eastside Made" artisan pop-up market for browsing local makers between sets.

It's the kind of low-key, walkable evening that's an easy yes just on proximity: Downtown Park is a short walk from most of our readers, it's free, and the variety show format means there's usually something worth catching even if you don't know the performers going in. Good one for an evening stroll downtown with no real commitment required.

Check the [event page](https://engage.oneredmond.org/events/arts-in-the-park) for the specific performer lineup each week before heading over.
