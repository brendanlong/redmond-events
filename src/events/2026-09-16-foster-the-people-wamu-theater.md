**Foster the People** — the indie-pop band behind "Pumped Up Kicks" and a run of hooky, synth-driven singles since — bring their **Good Mourning Sunshine Tour** to WaMu Theater on **Wednesday, September 16**, with **Goth Babe** opening. Doors are at 7 p.m., with the show starting around 8.

Squarely in the indie/pop lane readers tend to like, and a mid-size theater show rather than an arena — the kind of room where the sound and the crowd both feel more like a real concert than a spectacle.

WaMu Theater is down by Lumen Field, about a **30-minute drive from Redmond**. It's a weeknight, so budget for the commute in and the traffic home, but a well-known band in a solid venue on a Wednesday is a reasonable one to build an evening around.

Tickets at [wamutheater.com](https://www.wamutheater.com/events/foster-the-people-2026).
