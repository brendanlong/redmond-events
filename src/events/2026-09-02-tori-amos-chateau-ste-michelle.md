**Tori Amos** brings her **In Times of Dragons Tour** to the Chateau Ste. Michelle lawn on **Wednesday, September 2** — one of the more distinctive songwriters of the last three decades, known for piano-driven, genre-blurring records (*Little Earthquakes*, *Boys for Pele*) and a devoted following that treats her shows as something closer to ritual than concert. **Bartees Strange**, whose own music restlessly hops between indie rock, folk, and hip-hop, opens.

Gates open around **5:30 p.m.** for the **7 p.m.** start, standard winery-lawn format — bring a blanket or low chairs. Woodinville is a **15-20 minute drive** from downtown Redmond, an easy midweek trip if a Tori Amos show is the kind of thing that gets you excited (and for a lot of people, it very much is).

Tickets and full details at [ste-michelle.com](https://www.ste-michelle.com/concerts/summer-concerts), via Ticketmaster.
