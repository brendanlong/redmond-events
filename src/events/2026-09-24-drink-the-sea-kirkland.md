**Drink the Sea** is a genuine alt-rock supergroup playing Kirkland Performance Center on **Thursday, September 24 at 7pm**: Peter Buck (R.E.M.), Barrett Martin (Screaming Trees, Mad Season), Alain Johannes (Queens of the Stone Age, Eleven), Duke Garwood (Mark Lanegan Band), and Lisette Garcia.

That's a lot of Pacific Northwest rock pedigree in one room — the kind of lineup that would normally mean a trip into Seattle, but instead it's landing at a mid-sized Eastside theater. Right in the broad indie/alt-rock lane our readers go for, and different enough from a standard touring act to be worth the drive.

It's about a **15–20 minute drive to downtown Kirkland**, easy compared to fighting Seattle traffic and parking for a comparable show. Tickets ($45–$67, tiered) at [kpcenter.org](https://www.kpcenter.org/event/drink-the-sea/).
